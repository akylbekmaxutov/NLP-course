About the course

What this course covers

We start from raw text and simple patterns, move through classical Machine Learning and Word Embeddings, and finish with Transformers, Large Language Models, RAG, Agents, and Speech Processing.

Part 1

Working with text

Regular Expressions, Tokenization, Lemmatization, Bag of Words, and classical Machine Learning for text data.

Part 2

Learning meaning

Word Embeddings, word2vec, Text Classification, and the Attention mechanism behind the Transformer.

Part 3

Modern models

BERT, RoBERTa, Fine-tuning, Generative Models, Prompting, RAG, Agents, LLM, ASR, and TTS.
